# Lane selection

Choose `procedural-video` by default for Nous-style deterministic motion graphics: repeatable geometry, typography, diagrams, kinetic type, and local assets. Do not route that work to Kling.

Route only by invoking independent installed capabilities, never by copying their instructions: `social-video-deep-study` for reference research, `ascii-video` for text-grid visuals, `tldraw-offline` for diagram sources, `HyperFrames` when installed for its specialized frames, `ai-video-production`/generative lane only when a non-deterministic shot is explicitly needed, and `ai-music-production` for an original soundtrack. Missing optional capabilities must be recorded as unavailable and must not silently change the lane.
