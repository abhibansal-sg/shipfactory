ShipFactory — Work Model v0.2

3 STEP PROPERTIES · 1 COMPOSITION GRAMMAR · POLICY COMPUTED, NOT CHOSEN · supersedes the 6-axis draft (v0.1 killed under self-attack: A1 was grammar not axis · A2≡A4 double-count · A6 was derived policy · A3×A5 correlated · RISK was missing) · 23 Jul 2026

1 · A Step Has Exactly Three Properties

The unit of the model is the STEP, not the recipe (v0.1's category error — recipe rows were only fillable by lying). Every governed step, in any work category, is fully described by: what it makes, how it's checked, what it risks.

2 · Policy Is Computed, Not Chosen

v0.1 spent two axes (actor chain, failure route) on things nobody actually chooses. They are FUNCTIONS of the three properties. This is where governance lives — in one table, not in 300 lines of per-recipe prose.

3 · One Grammar Composes Steps · A Recipe Is a Sentence

Topology (v0.1's A1) was never an axis — it's the connective tissue. Four connectors compose steps into workflows. A recipe declares steps (PRODUCT + RISK) and wiring; the factory derives checks, gates, and routing from the policy table. dev-pipeline@14's 300 lines collapse to declarations.

What changed v0.1 → v0.2

  • • 6 axes → 3 step properties + 1 grammar + 1 policy table
  • • Unit of description: recipe → STEP (rows can be filled honestly)
  • • Actor chain + oracle merged: the checker IS the actor
  • • Failure routing + gate placement: now DERIVED, not declared
  • • RISK added — the governance axis v0.1 forgot
  • • Substrate folded into PRODUCT (they were ~1:1)

What this simplifies

  • • Recipe = PRODUCT+RISK declarations + wiring; checks/gates/routing computed
  • • dev-pipeline: 12 authored steps → ~6 declarations
  • • New work category = new sentence, zero new architecture
  • • Policy table is ONE versioned operator-owned artifact
  • • Seat/skill tuning keys on (PRODUCT, CHECK) pairs, not anecdote

Held invariant (unchanged)

  • • One Law: human approval gates, operator only; R3 ⇒ always gated
  • • Count-caps + deadlines only, never token budgets
  • • Immutable published recipes (@N+1)
  • • External effects only through the journaled-intent path
  • • Spine vs coordinate-machinery audit rule (kept from v0.1)
